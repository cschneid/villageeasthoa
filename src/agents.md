# Agents & Title Companies

The HOA is run by volunteer neighborhood members. We hold monthly meetings on operational matters, and an annual meeting in January at a local restaurant.

**There are no architectural review requirements.**

Primary expenditures are pool and park upkeep.

## Status Letters & Ownership Changes

- **Fee: $150** for HOA payment status letters and owner transfers
- **Contact:** [hoavillageeast@gmail.com](mailto:hoavillageeast@gmail.com)
- **Processing time:** 5–7 business days
