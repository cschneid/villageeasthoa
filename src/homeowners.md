# Home Owners

## Monthly Meetings

The board meets the **3rd Monday of each month at 6pm**.

- May–September: meetings held at the pool
- October–April: meetings rotate to board members' residences

## Annual Dues

- Payment deadline: **January**
- Late fees assessed after **March 1st**
- Annual statement arrives with the winter newsletter

### Dues cover:

- Pool & recreational facility access
- Lifeguard services
- Pool utilities (water, phone, electricity, waste removal)
- Pool maintenance and seasonal repairs
- Playground equipment maintenance
- Grounds maintenance (mowing, irrigation, fertilization)
- Tree care

## Pool Tags

- Distributed in spring with the summer newsletter
- Only sent to households current on dues
- **Lost tag replacement fee: $50**

## Community Standards

The HOA's authority is limited to the common areas and Association finances.
Property maintenance, nuisance, parking, and tree concerns are handled by the
City of Fort Collins — see
[Community Standards & Reporting Concerns](/neighborhood/standards/) for contacts
and reporting links.
