## About These Documents

The Bylaws and the Declaration of Covenants are the Association's binding
governing documents. The Responsible Governance Policies are posted for
reference and transparency.

The Association does not operate a covenant enforcement, architectural review,
or fine process for privately owned lots. For what the HOA does and does not
address — and where to direct property, nuisance, and tree concerns — see
[Community Standards & Reporting Concerns](/neighborhood/standards/).
