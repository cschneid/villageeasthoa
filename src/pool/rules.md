# Pool Rules & Documents

## Hours

### Summer Hours

- Sunday–Saturday: 10:30 AM – 8:30 PM
- Friday: extended until 10:00 PM

### School Year Hours

| Day | Hours |
|---|---|
| Sunday | 10:30 AM – 7:30 PM |
| Monday–Friday | 3:30 PM – 7:30 PM |
| Saturday | 10:30 AM – 7:30 PM |

## Pool Parties

Private parties are available for HOA members. The fee is $10, plus $30 per lifeguard.
