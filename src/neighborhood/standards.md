# Community Standards & Reporting Concerns

Village East is often asked whether the HOA enforces rules about how neighbors
keep up their property — yards, weeds, trash, parked or inoperable vehicles,
outdoor storage, or overgrown trees. **In almost every case, it does not.** Those
matters are handled by the City of Fort Collins, and residents can report them
directly.

## What the HOA Is Responsible For

Under our [Bylaws and Covenants](/documents/bylaws/), the Board's role is
narrow. Article VI ("Powers and Duties of the Board of Directors") gives the
Board authority to:

- Collect annual assessments and keep the Association's financial records
- Maintain the **common areas** — the pool, park, green space, playground
  equipment, and courts
- Adopt and enforce rules governing **use of the common areas** (for example,
  the pool rules) and suspend common-area privileges for unpaid dues or rule
  violations
- Carry insurance, hire contractors, and issue status certificates

Our covenants do **not** give the Association authority over lawn care, weeds,
trash, junk or inoperable vehicles, parking, home repairs in driveways, paint
colors, or landscaping on privately owned lots. Village East has never adopted
community standards, design guidelines, or property maintenance rules for
privately owned lots, and the Board does not issue citations or fines over those
issues. Village East is simply not that kind of HOA.

### About architectural control

Article V of the Declaration ("Architectural Control") gives the Board the
*authority* to review plans for new buildings, fences, walls, other structures,
and exterior alterations. Residents sometimes ask whether that means Village
East has design rules. It does not.

**The Board has not adopted any architectural standards, design guidelines, or
review procedures, and does not operate an architectural review process.** There
is no approved materials list, no color palette, no fence specification, and no
committee reviewing submissions. The authority exists in the covenants but has
never been exercised through adopted policy.

In practice, this means the binding requirements for a fence, shed, deck, or
addition in Village East are the City of Fort Collins building and zoning
requirements, not HOA rules. Check permit requirements with the City before you
build:
[Building Services](https://www.fortcollins.gov/Services/Building-Services)
or Zoning at 970-416-2745.

Article V also provides that if plans are submitted to the Board and the Board
does not approve or disapprove them within 30 days, approval is not required and
the article is deemed satisfied. Homeowners who want that on the record for a
future sale are welcome to email plans to
[hoavillageeast@gmail.com](mailto:hoavillageeast@gmail.com), but the Association
does not require it.

## Where to Report Property & Nuisance Concerns

The City of Fort Collins enforces its municipal code throughout the
neighborhood. Reports can be filed online, by phone, or by email, and the City
accepts anonymous reports (though anonymity may limit their follow-up).

### Access Fort Collins — online reporting

The City's reporting portal routes your report to the correct department:

**<https://fortcollinsco.citysourced.com/>**

There are also free mobile apps for
[iOS](https://itunes.apple.com/app/id6743873767) and
[Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.citysourced.fortcollinsco).

### City Code Compliance

Code Compliance handles most neighborhood property complaints.

- **Phone:** 970-416-2350 (Mon–Fri, 8 a.m. – 5 p.m.)
- **Email:** [CodeCompliance@fortcollins.gov](mailto:CodeCompliance@fortcollins.gov)
- **Office:** 281 N. College Ave., Fort Collins, CO 80524
- **Web:** <https://www.fortcollins.gov/Services/Housing/In-Your-Neighborhood/Code-Compliance>

Code Compliance handles weeds and unmowed grass, trash and rubbish,
improper outdoor storage, overgrown trees and shrubs, unshoveled sidewalks,
right-of-way encroachment, construction noise and dust, and inoperable or
improperly parked vehicles on private property.

The City's own guidance notes that vehicles and trailers must be parked on
gravel, concrete, asphalt, or a similar hard improved surface, and that
inoperable vehicles must be kept in a garage or screened from view.

### Overhanging branches over sidewalks

This is a common concern in Village East, and the City code is specific.
Fort Collins Municipal Code Sec. 27-57 makes the **property owner** responsible
for trimming trees, shrubs, and other woody vegetation on their property so that
it does not encroach on a public sidewalk, street, or alley. Branches must be
maintained at a height of **at least 8 feet over a public sidewalk** and at
least 14 feet over travel lanes. If an owner does not comply, the City Forester
may serve notice requiring the work be done.

If you notice branches making a sidewalk hard to pass with a stroller,
wheelchair, or dog, report it through Access Fort Collins under "Overgrown Trees
& Shrubs," or call Code Compliance.

## A Note From the Board

We appreciate that residents care about how the neighborhood looks and feels —
that concern is what keeps Village East a good place to live. The Board is a
small group of volunteers whose authority is limited to the common areas and the
Association's finances. Pointing you toward the City is not a brush-off; it is
genuinely the office with the tools and legal authority to address these issues.

A friendly conversation with a neighbor also resolves many concerns faster than
any formal process, and we'd encourage that as a first step where you feel
comfortable.

Questions about what the Association does or does not cover are always welcome
at [hoavillageeast@gmail.com](mailto:hoavillageeast@gmail.com).
